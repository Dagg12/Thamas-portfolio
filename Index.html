About Us

Who We Are

THAMAS TECH WORLD is a South African technology company established in March 2026. Although we are a growing business, we are passionate about providing reliable networking solutions for homes and businesses.

We specialize in designing, installing, configuring and optimizing wireless networks that provide stable, secure and high-performance internet connectivity.

We believe every customer deserves reliable WiFi coverage without dead zones or poor signal strength. Our goal is to deliver professional workmanship while continuously improving our knowledge of modern networking technologies.

What areas do you serve?

How long does a typical installation take?
Most residential installations are completed within 1–3 hours, depending on the size of the property and the complexity of the setup.
Do you offer support after installation?
Yes! We provide ongoing support and maintenance to ensure your network continues to perform optimally.
Can you upgrade my existing network?
Absolutely. We specialize in upgrading and optimizing existing networks to improve performance and coverage.
